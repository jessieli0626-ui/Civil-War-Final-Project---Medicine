Additional Nitrogen Connections

These additional connections show how nitrogen relates to chemistry, biology, agriculture, and industrial processes.

Nitrogen & Living Things: Nitrogen is essential for proteins, amino acids, and DNA. Plants and bacteria help convert nitrogen into forms living things can use.
Haber Process: The Haber process made nitrogen extremely important because it allowed nitrogen from air to be turned into ammonia for fertilizer.
Oxidation Numbers: Nitrogen commonly has oxidation numbers such as -3, +3, and +5, allowing it to form many different compounds.
